# Release Agreement

This agreement is made between **The Moon Records** ("Label") and **{{artist_name}}**{{artist_address}} ("Artist"), regarding the release **{{release_title}}** (catalog no. {{catalog}}, scheduled for {{release_date}}) (the "Release").

## 1. Grant of Rights

1.1 The Artist grants the Label the exclusive, worldwide right to distribute, stream, promote and make available the Release (all tracks and artwork delivered for it) on all digital platforms, for a term of **{{term_months}} months** starting on the release date (the "Term").

1.2 The Artist remains the owner of the premaster recordings and all underlying rights. This agreement is a licence, not a transfer of ownership.

1.3 The Label may use the Artist's name, likeness, biography and the Release artwork to promote the Release, on its website, social media, newsletters and promotional material.

1.4 **Artist's own Bandcamp.** Notwithstanding the exclusivity in 1.1, the Artist may also upload, offer and sell the Release on the Artist's own Bandcamp account. Revenue from the Artist's own Bandcamp account belongs entirely to the Artist; section 4 does not apply to it, and the Label has no claim to it.

## 2. Delivery

2.1 The Artist delivers final, premastered audio files in the formats the Label reasonably requests.

2.2 The Label decides release date, format, metadata and distribution channels, in consultation with the Artist.

## 3. Artist Warranties

The Artist confirms that:

- they are the sole creator and rights holder of the Release, or have all necessary permissions from every contributor;
- all samples, loops and third-party material used are cleared or royalty-free;
- the Release does not infringe any third-party rights;
- they have not granted and will not grant conflicting rights in the Release during the Term.

## 4. Revenue and Payouts

4.1 **Other platforms.** Revenue from streaming platforms such as SoundCloud, and payments processed through PayPal, is split automatically at the source (split payment). The Artist's share is paid directly to the Artist by the respective platform. The Label does not handle, collect or forward these payments and has no payout obligations regarding them.

4.2 **Bandcamp.** The Artist receives **{{royalty_rate}}%** of the net revenue from sales of the Release on the Label's Bandcamp account (after deduction of Bandcamp's and the payment provider's fees).

4.3 **Minimum payout.** The Artist's Bandcamp share is paid out once the accumulated, not yet paid balance reaches **EUR 50**. Payouts are made via Bandcamp. Balances below this threshold are carried forward and added to later sales; they do not expire during the Term.

4.4 **End of Term.** If a balance below EUR 50 remains when the Term ends, the Label pays it out upon the Artist's written request.

4.5 **Statements.** The Artist may request a Bandcamp sales overview for the Release once per year.

## 5. Credits

The Label credits the Artist as the creator of the Release on all platforms where crediting is technically possible, and lists The Moon Records as the label.

## 6. Term and Termination

6.1 The agreement runs for the Term. Unless either party objects in writing at least 30 days before it ends, it is not renewed.

6.2 Either party may terminate the agreement for good cause with written notice, in particular if the other party materially breaches it and does not remedy the breach within 30 days of written notice.

6.3 After the Term ends, or upon termination, the Label stops actively distributing the Release and requests takedown from platforms within a reasonable period (up to 60 days). Rights to already-earned royalties remain unaffected.

## 7. Liability

Each party is liable to the other according to statutory provisions. The Artist indemnifies the Label against third-party claims arising from a breach of the warranties in section 3.

## 8. General Provisions

8.1 This agreement is the entire agreement between the parties regarding the Release. Changes must be made in writing (text form is sufficient).

8.2 If a provision is or becomes invalid, the remaining provisions stay in effect.

8.3 This agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Germany.

## 9. Electronic Signature

Both parties agree that this agreement may be concluded by simple electronic signature. The Artist signs by entering their full name on the signing page; the Label records the time of signing, IP address and a hash of the agreement text as evidence.
